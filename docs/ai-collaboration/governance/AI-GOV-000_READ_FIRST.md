# Purpose

This document is the mandatory entry point for every AI participating in the development of AEVON.

Its purpose is to provide a consistent onboarding process and direct every AI to the appropriate governance documents before contributing to the project.

No engineering work shall begin until the relevant documents have been reviewed.

---

# Scope

This document defines:

- The purpose of the AI Collaboration Governance package.
- The required reading order.
- Which documents are mandatory.
- Which documents are situational.
- The expected onboarding behaviour of every AI.

This document does not define engineering principles, operating rules, project context, or current development activities.

---

# AI Collaboration Governance Package

The AI Collaboration Governance package enables any AI assistant to contribute to AEVON safely, consistently, and independently of previous conversation history.

Its objectives are to:

- Establish a consistent operating model.
- Preserve engineering continuity.
- Support collaboration across AI platforms and accounts.
- Enable seamless project migration.
- Minimize dependency on conversational memory.

# Expected Behaviour

Every AI participating in AEVON shall:

- Read all mandatory governance documents.
- Read any additional documents relevant to the current task.
- Request clarification whenever required information is unavailable or inconsistent.
- Never assume undocumented engineering decisions.
- Follow the approved governance before contributing.

---

# Completion Criteria

An AI is considered ready to contribute when it:

- Understands its role.
- Has reviewed the required governance documents.
- Has sufficient project context.
- Knows the current engineering objective.
- Is prepared to follow the established collaboration process.
