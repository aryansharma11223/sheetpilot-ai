# Purpose

This template records the outcome of an engineering session and prepares the next AI or engineer to continue work without losing context.

Its purpose is to preserve continuity between engineering sessions while minimizing the need to reconstruct previous discussions or decisions.

---

# Scope

This template applies to the conclusion of every engineering session.

It captures:

- Session objective.
- Work completed.
- Decisions made.
- Remaining work.
- Known blockers.
- Recommended next action.

It does not replace project documentation, governance, or milestone planning.
