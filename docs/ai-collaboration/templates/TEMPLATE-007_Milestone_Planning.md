# Purpose

This template provides a standardized approach for planning engineering milestones within AEVON.

Its purpose is to ensure every milestone begins with a clearly defined objective, scope, deliverables, dependencies, risks, and completion criteria.

Well-planned milestones reduce ambiguity, improve engineering focus, and establish measurable outcomes.
