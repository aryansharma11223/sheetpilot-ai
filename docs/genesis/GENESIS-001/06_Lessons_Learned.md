## Key Learnings

A strong architectural foundation begins with clearly defined principles rather
than implementation.

Timeless engineering decisions reduce future architectural drift.

The Constitution should evolve only through a structured amendment process.

Documentation is a first-class engineering artifact and shall evolve alongside
the platform.

Every future milestone should follow the same engineering lifecycle established
during Genesis.
