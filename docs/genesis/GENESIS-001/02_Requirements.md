# ===============================================================================
# GENESIS-001 — REQUIREMENTS
# ===============================================================================

## Functional Requirements

The Constitution shall define:

- Preamble
- Vision
- Mission
- Long-Term Purpose
- Constitutional Principles
- Engineering Principles
- Evolution Principles
- Human Governance
- Knowledge Philosophy
- Independence Principles
- Security Principles
- Quality Standards
- Definition of Success
- Constitutional Amendment Process

## Non-Functional Requirements

The Constitution shall:

- remain timeless
- remain technology independent
- remain vendor independent
- remain understandable by humans and AI
- remain self-contained
- support future evolution

## Acceptance Criteria

- Constitution completed
- Founder recorded
- Governance documented
- Evolution principles documented
- Amendment process defined
