# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Knowledge Evolution Framework for the AEVON Platform.

The framework defines how organizational knowledge continuously grows, adapts, improves, and matures throughout its lifecycle, ensuring that enterprise knowledge remains accurate, relevant, valuable, and aligned with changing business needs, engineering practices, and artificial intelligence capabilities.

Knowledge is a living asset that must continuously evolve.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Knowledge Objects
- Enterprise Knowledge Graph
- AI Memory
- Engineering Standards
- Documentation
- Source Code Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- Business Knowledge
- Lessons Learned
- FORGE
- AI Agents

---

# Vision

To establish a continuously learning enterprise where every engineering activity contributes to the growth and improvement of organizational intelligence.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Knowledge should never become obsolete.

Every project, decision, success, failure, review, and innovation contributes to stronger organizational intelligence.

Knowledge evolution is continuous—not periodic.

---

# Objectives

The framework shall:

- Continuously improve enterprise knowledge.
- Capture organizational learning.
- Prevent knowledge decay.
- Promote innovation.
- Preserve institutional memory.
- Improve AI reasoning.
- Strengthen engineering decisions.
- Increase organizational intelligence.

- Technology changes
- Standard revisions
- Business changes
