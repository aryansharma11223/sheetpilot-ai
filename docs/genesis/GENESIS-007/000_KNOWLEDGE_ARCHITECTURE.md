# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Knowledge Architecture for the AEVON Platform.

Knowledge is the most valuable engineering asset within AEVON. Every engineering decision, AI response, software component, project artifact, standard, and lesson learned ultimately depends on the quality, accessibility, and governance of organizational knowledge.

This document defines how knowledge is represented, structured, connected, governed, discovered, and evolved throughout the platform.

---

# Scope

This architecture applies to:

- Engineering Knowledge
- Business Knowledge
- AI Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- Architecture Knowledge
- Standards
- Documentation
- Source Code Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Organizational Memory
- FORGE
- AI Agents
- Future Knowledge Services

---

# Vision

To establish a unified enterprise knowledge ecosystem where information is transformed into structured, governed, searchable, reusable, and continuously evolving organizational intelligence.

Knowledge shall become an engineering asset rather than a collection of disconnected documents.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Knowledge is not documentation.

Knowledge is the relationship between information, context, experience, decisions, and understanding.

Documentation stores information.

Knowledge Architecture enables intelligence.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Architecture shall:

- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Enable rapid discovery.
- Improve AI reasoning.
- Support engineering decisions.
- Eliminate duplicated knowledge.
- Promote knowledge reuse.
- Enable semantic understanding.
- Support autonomous engineering.
- Maintain governance and traceability.
- Continuously improve organizational intelligence.

# Knowledge Layers

The AEVON Knowledge Architecture consists of six logical layers.

## Layer 1 — Information

Raw facts, files, records, and data.

---

## Layer 2 — Documents

Structured documentation and engineering artifacts.

---

## Layer 3 — Knowledge Objects

Discrete engineering knowledge units with metadata and relationships.

---

## Layer 4 — Semantic Knowledge

Meaningful relationships established through ontologies and graphs.

---

## Layer 5 — Organizational Intelligence

Validated, governed, reusable engineering knowledge.

---

## Layer 6 — Engineering Wisdom

Strategic insight generated through accumulated organizational learning.
