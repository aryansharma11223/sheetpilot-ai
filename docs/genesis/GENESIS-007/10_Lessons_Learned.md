# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Lessons Learned Framework for the AEVON Platform.

The framework defines how engineering experience, project outcomes, operational events, successes, failures, innovations, and retrospective findings are systematically captured, validated, governed, shared, and transformed into reusable organizational knowledge.

Lessons Learned ensure that organizational experience becomes organizational intelligence.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Engineering Projects
- Software Development
- AI Systems
- Infrastructure
- Operations
- Incident Management
- Architecture Reviews
- Quality Assurance
- Security Events
- Research Activities
- Business Operations
- FORGE
- AI Agents

---

# Vision

To ensure that every significant organizational experience contributes to continuous learning and strengthens future engineering decisions.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Experience has little value if it is forgotten.

Lessons become valuable only when they are:

- Captured
- Validated
- Shared
- Reused
- Embedded into future engineering practices

Learning is an engineering process—not an accidental outcome.

---

# Objectives

The framework shall:

- Capture organizational experience.
- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Prevent repeated mistakes.
- Promote best practices.
- Accelerate engineering maturity.
- Improve AI reasoning.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Strengthen organizational resilience.

# Lesson Structure

Each Lesson Learned shall contain:

- Lesson ID
- Title
- Description
- Context
- Problem Statement
- Root Cause
- Resolution
- Outcome
- Recommendations
- Related Knowledge Objects
- Related Projects
- Author
- Reviewer
- Approval Status
- Version
- Classification
- Keywords
- Date Captured

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Where applicable, lessons shall identify:

- Immediate Cause
- Contributing Factors
- Systemic Causes
- Process Weaknesses
- Technical Deficiencies
- Human Factors

Root causes shall focus on systemic improvement rather than assigning blame.

---

# Validation

Lessons shall be validated through:

- Technical Review
- Business Review
- Evidence Verification
- Engineering Assessment
- Governance Approval

Only validated lessons become enterprise knowledge.

---

# Classification

Lessons shall be classified using:

- Domain
- Engineering Discipline
- Project
- Technology
- Risk Category
- Business Function
- Severity
- Reusability

Classification supports efficient retrieval.

---

# Knowledge Graph Integration

Approved lessons shall become Knowledge Objects within the Enterprise Knowledge Graph.

Relationships may include:

- Related Projects
- Related Standards
- Related Components
- Similar Lessons
- Root Causes
- Engineering Decisions
- AI Memory
- Best Practices

Lessons become part of organizational intelligence.

# AI Integration

FORGE and AI Agents shall:

- Recommend relevant lessons
- Detect recurring patterns

- Provide contextual lessons during engineering activities

- Improve AI reasoning using validated experience
