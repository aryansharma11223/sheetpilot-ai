# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Knowledge Governance Framework for the AEVON Platform.

Knowledge Governance defines the policies, roles, responsibilities, controls, and processes that ensure enterprise knowledge remains accurate, trusted, secure, compliant, and continuously maintained throughout its lifecycle.

Governance transforms knowledge into a reliable organizational asset.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Enterprise Knowledge Repositories
- Knowledge Objects
- Knowledge Graph
- AI Memory
- Engineering Standards
- Documentation
- Source Code Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- AI Agents
- FORGE
- All Organizational Knowledge Assets

---

# Vision

To establish a governed knowledge ecosystem where every knowledge asset is trustworthy, traceable, secure, and managed according to enterprise engineering standards.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Knowledge without governance becomes misinformation.

Governance without knowledge limits innovation.

Effective governance balances:

- Trust
- Accessibility
- Accountability
- Security
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Objectives

The framework shall:

- Establish ownership.
- Ensure accountability.
- Maintain knowledge quality.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Enable compliance.
- Support auditability.
- Preserve traceability.
- Improve organizational trust.

---

# Knowledge Ownership

Every Knowledge Object shall have:

- Business Owner
- Technical Owner
- Reviewer
- Approver
- Custodian

Knowledge without ownership is prohibited.

---

# Approval Workflow

Every governed knowledge asset follows:

```text
Draft
   │
Technical Review
   │
Business Review
   │
Approval
   │
Publication
   │
Periodic Review
   │
Revision
   │
Archive
```

Approval shall be documented.

---

# Version Management

Knowledge shall support:

- Major Versions
- Minor Versions
- Revision History
- Change Logs
- Rollback Capability
- Historical Records

Previous versions shall remain traceable.

---

# Review Cycles

Knowledge shall be reviewed based on:

- Business Criticality
- Regulatory Requirements
- Engineering Changes
- Project Milestones
- AI Recommendations

Critical knowledge shall be reviewed more frequently.

---

# Knowledge Quality

Governance shall ensure:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Currency
- Relevance
- Traceability
- Discoverability

Quality standards apply before publication.

---

# Compliance

Knowledge governance shall support:

- Internal Policies
- Engineering Standards
- Industry Standards
- Regulatory Requirements
- Contractual Obligations
- Audit Requirements

Compliance shall be measurable.

---

# Security Classification

Knowledge shall be classified according to sensitivity.

Example classifications:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted
- Highly Restricted

Security classifications determine access permissions.

---

# Access Control

Knowledge access shall follow:

- Least Privilege
- Need-to-Know
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC)

Every access request shall be auditable.

---

# Audit Trail

The platform shall record:

- Creation
- Modification
- Review
- Approval
- Publication
- Access
- Deletion
- Restoration

Audit records shall be immutable.

---

# Retention Policy

Knowledge shall follow defined retention schedules.

Retention depends upon:

- Business Value
- Legal Requirements
- Engineering Importance
- Historical Significance

Archived knowledge shall remain searchable where permitted.

---

# Risk Management

Governance shall mitigate risks including:

- Outdated Knowledge
- Duplicate Knowledge
- Unauthorized Access
- Missing Ownership
- Data Loss
- AI Hallucinations
- Incorrect Context
- Compliance Violations

Each risk shall have documented mitigation measures.

---

# Governance Metrics

The platform shall monitor:

- Knowledge Quality Score
- Review Completion Rate
- Approval Time
- Compliance Rate
- Metadata Completeness
- Ownership Coverage
- Duplicate Detection Rate
- Repository Health

Metrics drive governance improvements.

---

# Integration with FORGE

FORGE shall automate:

- Ownership validation
- Metadata verification
- Governance compliance
- Duplicate detection
- Review reminders
- Approval workflows
- Audit reporting
- Governance dashboards

Human approval remains mandatory for authoritative knowledge.

---

# Exception Management

Governance exceptions shall include:

- Exception Identifier
- Justification
- Risk Assessment
- Approval Authority
- Expiration Date
- Corrective Actions

Exceptions shall be temporary and reviewed regularly.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Governance processes shall improve through:

- Audit Findings
- Lessons Learned
- Engineering Feedback
- AI Recommendations
- Performance Metrics
- Process Reviews

Governance itself shall evolve over time.

The Enterprise Knowledge Governance Framework establishes the policies, controls, ownership model, and lifecycle processes required to maintain trusted organizational knowledge within AEVON.

By combining governance, quality management, security, compliance, lifecycle management, and FORGE-powered automation, the framework ensures that enterprise knowledge remains accurate, discoverable, auditable, and fit for both Human Engineers and AI Agents.

Knowledge Governance is the trust layer that enables AEVON's Knowledge Architecture to scale confidently while preserving integrity and engineering excellence.
