# Project Identity - Requirements

## Functional Requirements

The Project Identity shall define:

- Identity Statement
- Vision
- Mission
- Founder
- Ownership
- Core Values
- Engineering Philosophy
- Evolution Philosophy
- Versioning Strategy
- Repository Philosophy

---

# Governance Requirements

The identity shall:

- Respect the Constitution.
- Preserve the Founder's vision.
- Require documented approval for major changes.

---

# Documentation Requirements

The identity document shall be:

- Self-contained.
- Version controlled.
- Human readable.
- AI readable.
- Technology independent.

---

# Quality Requirements

The document shall be:

- Complete
- Consistent
- Traceable
- Maintainable
- Future-proof

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

The identity shall remain:

- Stable
- Explainable
- Extensible
- Understandable

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The milestone is complete when:

- All mandatory sections exist.
- Founder is documented.
- Vision is documented.
- Mission is documented.
- Philosophy is documented.
- Governance is defined.
- Constitution alignment is verified.
