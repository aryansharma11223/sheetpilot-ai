# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Quality Assurance (QA) Framework for the AEVON Platform.

Quality Assurance provides the governance, methodologies, standards, processes, audits, measurements, and continuous improvement practices necessary to ensure that every engineering activity consistently delivers products and services that meet enterprise expectations.

Unlike testing, which identifies defects in delivered software, Quality Assurance focuses on preventing defects by improving engineering processes.

---

# Scope

Quality Assurance applies to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Software Engineering
- AI Engineering
- Infrastructure Engineering
- Platform Services
- Security Engineering
- DevOps
- Documentation
- Operational Processes
- Project Governance

---

# Vision

To establish a culture where quality is built into every engineering activity through standardized processes, objective measurement, continuous improvement, and organizational accountability.

---

# Quality Philosophy

Quality shall be:

- Planned
- Engineered
- Measured
- Audited
- Improved
- Governed
- Automated where practical
- Continuously Assessed

Quality is the responsibility of every contributor.

---

# Objectives

Quality Assurance shall:

- Standardize engineering practices.
- Prevent defects.
- Improve engineering maturity.
- Increase delivery consistency.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Strengthen customer confidence.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Support enterprise governance.

# Quality Planning

Quality planning shall define:

- Quality Objectives
- Acceptance Criteria
- Review Requirements
- Testing Strategy
- Metrics
- Compliance Requirements
- Improvement Targets

Quality planning shall begin before implementation.

---

# Process Assurance

Process assurance shall verify compliance with approved:

- Development Standards
- Architecture Standards
- Coding Standards
- Security Policies
- AI Governance
- Operational Procedures
- Documentation Standards

Process deviations shall be documented and resolved.

---

# Quality Control

Quality Control shall verify engineering outputs through:

- Reviews
- Inspections
- Testing
- Validation
- Audits
- Metrics Analysis

Quality Control confirms that deliverables satisfy defined requirements.

---

# Engineering Reviews

Mandatory engineering reviews include:

- Requirements Review
- Architecture Review
- Design Review
- Code Review
- Security Review
- AI Review
- Deployment Review
- Operational Readiness Review

Review outcomes shall be formally recorded.

---

# Quality Audits

Quality audits shall assess:

- Process Compliance
- Standards Compliance
- Documentation Completeness
- Evidence Availability
- Traceability
- Governance Effectiveness

Audit findings shall include corrective actions.

---

# Defect Prevention

Preventive practices include:

- Architecture Reviews
- Coding Standards
- Static Analysis
- Secure Development Practices
- Automated Testing
- Knowledge Sharing
- Root Cause Analysis

Preventing defects is preferred over correcting defects.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Significant defects shall undergo structured analysis.

Root cause investigations shall identify:

- Process Gaps
- Technical Causes
- Human Factors
- Tool Limitations
- Training Needs

Corrective actions shall be tracked to completion.

---

# Quality Metrics

Quality shall be measured using:

- Defect Density
- Escaped Defects
- Test Coverage
- Automation Coverage
- Code Quality Index
- Customer Satisfaction
- Audit Findings
- Process Compliance
- MTTR
- Change Failure Rate

Metrics shall be reviewed regularly.

---

# Continuous Quality Improvement

Continuous improvement activities shall include:

- Retrospectives
- Lessons Learned
- Process Refinement
- Automation Expansion
- Knowledge Sharing
- Benchmarking
- Innovation Initiatives

Quality maturity shall improve incrementally.

---

# Documentation Quality

Documentation shall be:

- Complete
- Accurate
- Version Controlled
- Reviewable
- Traceable
- Accessible

Documentation quality is part of product quality.

---

# AI Quality Assurance

AI quality assurance shall evaluate:

- Prompt Libraries
- Knowledge Accuracy
- Response Quality
- Hallucination Trends
- Safety Controls
- Human Review Outcomes
- AI Governance Compliance

AI quality shall be monitored continuously.

# Supplier Quality

Third-party software, APIs, models, and services shall be evaluated for:

- Reliability
- Security
- Performance
- Licensing
- Compliance
- Operational Support

Supplier quality shall be reviewed periodically.

Quality governance shall include:

- Improvement Initiatives
- Quality Metrics

# Summary

The Enterprise Quality Assurance Framework establishes the governance, standards, audits, metrics, and continuous improvement practices required to achieve engineering excellence across the AEVON Platform.

By integrating quality planning, process assurance, defect prevention, engineering reviews, audit programs, measurable quality indicators, AI quality management, and organizational governance, AEVON ensures that quality is embedded throughout the engineering lifecycle rather than evaluated only after implementation.

This framework provides the organizational foundation for sustainable, measurable, and continuously improving enterprise quality.
