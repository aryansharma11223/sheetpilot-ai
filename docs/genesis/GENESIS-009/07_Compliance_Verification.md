# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Compliance Verification Standard for the AEVON Platform.

Compliance Verification ensures that all software, AI systems, infrastructure, operational processes, engineering practices, and organizational controls conform to applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, internal policies, industry standards, and enterprise governance requirements.

The objective is to maintain continuous compliance while minimizing legal, operational, financial, and reputational risks.

---

# Scope

Compliance Verification applies to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Software Engineering
- AI Systems
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Information Security
- Data Governance
- Privacy Controls
- Operational Processes
- Third-Party Services
- Business Continuity
- Documentation

---

# Vision

To establish a continuously monitored compliance framework that provides objective evidence of adherence to enterprise policies, regulatory obligations, and industry standards throughout the platform lifecycle.

---

# Compliance Philosophy

Compliance shall be:

- Continuous
- Evidence-Based
- Risk-Oriented
- Auditable
- Automated where practical
- Transparent
- Traceable
- Governed

Compliance shall be integrated into engineering workflows rather than performed only during audits.

---

# Objectives

Compliance Verification shall:

- Verify regulatory compliance.
- Validate internal policy adherence.
- Reduce compliance risk.
- Improve audit readiness.
- Protect enterprise assets.
- Support customer trust.
- Strengthen governance.
- Enable continuous compliance monitoring.

# Regulatory Verification

Verification activities shall confirm compliance with applicable:

- National Laws
- Industry Regulations
- Contractual Requirements
- Corporate Policies
- Customer Obligations
- Internal Standards

Applicable regulations shall be periodically reviewed.

---

# Control Verification

Enterprise controls shall be verified for:

- Design Effectiveness
- Operational Effectiveness
- Consistent Execution
- Traceability
- Evidence Availability
- Exception Handling

Control failures shall trigger corrective actions.

---

# AI Compliance Verification

AI governance verification shall assess:

- Responsible AI Policies
- Prompt Governance
- Model Approval
- Human Oversight
- Data Usage Compliance
- Explainability
- Safety Controls
- Auditability

AI compliance shall be reviewed before production deployment.

---

# Security Compliance

Security compliance verification shall include:

- Access Controls
- Identity Management
- Encryption
- Logging
- Vulnerability Management
- Secure Configuration
- Incident Response
- Security Monitoring

Security evidence shall be retained for audit purposes.

---

# Data Governance Verification

Verification shall confirm:

- Data Classification
- Data Retention
- Data Integrity
- Backup Validation
- Privacy Controls
- Data Access Policies
- Data Lifecycle Management

Sensitive information shall receive enhanced protection.

---

# Documentation Compliance

Required documentation shall include:

- Policies
- Standards
- Procedures
- Architecture Records
- Verification Reports
- Audit Evidence

- Approval Records

# Audit Readiness

Audit readiness shall ensure:

- Complete Evidence
- Traceability
- Document Availability
- Control Validation
- Corrective Action Records
- Approval History

Audit preparation shall be continuous rather than event-driven.

---

# Exception Management

Compliance exceptions shall include:

- Business Justification
- Risk Assessment
- Temporary Controls
- Approval Authority
- Expiration Date
- Review Schedule

Exceptions shall not become permanent without formal review.

---

- Compliance Score

- Open Exceptions

- Policy Compliance Rate
- AI Governance Compliance

Metrics shall support executive governance.
