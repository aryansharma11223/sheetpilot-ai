# Purpose

This document defines the fundamental principles that govern every engineering workflow within the AEVON Platform.

Regardless of whether the work is performed by Human Engineers, AI Engineers, or FORGE, all engineering activities shall comply with these principles to ensure consistency, quality, traceability, and long-term maintainability.

These principles form the operational philosophy of engineering execution and apply across all engineering disciplines.

---

# Scope

These principles apply to:

- Software Engineering
- AI Engineering
- Platform Engineering
- Infrastructure Engineering
- Documentation
- Knowledge Engineering
- Automation
- Testing
- Release Management
- Operations
- Research
- Future Engineering Disciplines

---

# Objectives

The Workflow Principles shall:

- Standardize engineering execution.
- Improve collaboration.
- Increase engineering quality.
- Support repeatability.
- Enable automation.
- Preserve engineering knowledge.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Encourage continuous improvement.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Engineering success is achieved through disciplined workflows rather than individual effort.

Every engineering activity should:

- Follow an approved workflow.
- Produce measurable outcomes.
- Be transparent.
- Be traceable.
- Be reviewable.
- Be continuously improved.

The workflow is considered the primary engineering asset.

---

# Fundamental Principles

---

## WFP-001 — Workflow Before Work

No engineering activity shall begin without following an approved workflow.

Engineering should never depend solely on personal habits or undocumented practices.

---

## WFP-002 — Standardization

Engineering workflows shall be standardized wherever practical.

Standardization:

- Improves quality.
- Simplifies onboarding.
- Reduces variation.
- Enables automation.

---

## WFP-003 — Repeatability

Every workflow should be repeatable.

Given the same inputs, the workflow should consistently produce equivalent outputs.

Repeatability ensures reliability.

---

## WFP-004 — Traceability

Every engineering activity shall be traceable.

Traceability shall exist between:

- Requirements
- Architecture
- ADRs
- Source Code
- Tests
- Documentation
- Releases

Engineering history shall never be lost.

---

## WFP-005 — Collaboration

Engineering is collaborative.

Responsibilities are shared between:

- Human Engineers
- AI Engineers
- FORGE

Each participant contributes according to its strengths while maintaining clear accountability.

---

## WFP-006 — Quality by Design

Quality shall be built into every engineering stage.

Quality is not a final inspection activity.

Every workflow stage contributes to engineering excellence.

---

## WFP-007 — Automation First

Whenever engineering activities become repetitive, they should be automated.

Automation should:

- Reduce manual effort.
- Improve consistency.
- Preserve traceability.
- Maintain human oversight.

---

## WFP-008 — Simplicity

Engineering workflows should remain as simple as possible while satisfying engineering objectives.

Unnecessary complexity should be eliminated.

---

## WFP-009 — Continuous Learning

Every completed workflow should contribute to organizational knowledge.

Lessons learned shall improve future workflows.

Engineering maturity grows through accumulated experience.

---

## WFP-010 — Continuous Improvement

Engineering workflows shall evolve continuously.

Improvements may originate from:

- Engineering metrics
- Retrospectives
- Operational feedback
- AI recommendations
- Architectural evolution

No workflow is permanently complete.

---

# Workflow Characteristics

Every workflow should demonstrate the following characteristics:

- Repeatable
- Predictable
- Observable
- Measurable
- Governed
- Secure
- Versioned
- Scalable
- Modular
- Maintainable

# Workflow Lifecycle

Every workflow progresses through a consistent lifecycle.

```text
Initiate
    │
Plan
    │
Execute
    │
Review
    │
Approve
    │
Deliver
    │
Improve
```

Individual workflows may extend this lifecycle but shall not contradict it.
