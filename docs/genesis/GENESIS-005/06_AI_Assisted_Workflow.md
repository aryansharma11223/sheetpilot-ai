# Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence participates within the AEVON Engineering Workflow.

Rather than replacing engineers, AI functions as an Engineering Partner that accelerates engineering activities while preserving human responsibility, architectural integrity, engineering quality, and governance.

The AI-Assisted Workflow establishes standardized collaboration between Human Engineers, AI Engineers, and FORGE throughout the complete engineering lifecycle.

---

# Scope

This workflow applies to AI participation in:

- Requirements Engineering
- Architecture Design
- Software Development
- Documentation
- Testing
- Code Reviews
- Quality Assurance
- Security Analysis
- Knowledge Management
- Engineering Research
- Operations
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Objectives

The AI-Assisted Workflow shall:

- Accelerate engineering activities.
- Improve engineering quality.
- Reduce repetitive manual work.
- Preserve engineering knowledge.
- Improve consistency.
- Maintain engineering governance.
- Support traceability.
- Enable responsible AI adoption.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

AI is an Engineering Collaborator.

AI shall:

- Assist.
- Recommend.
- Analyze.
- Generate.
- Validate.

AI shall never independently:

- Approve releases.
- Approve architectures.
- Override engineering standards.
- Modify production systems without authorization.
- Replace engineering accountability.

Human engineers remain responsible for all engineering decisions.

# AI Workflow

```text
Engineering Task
        │

        │

        │

        │

        │
Engineering Validation
        │
```

Every AI interaction shall produce traceable engineering evidence.

---

# AI Context Management

Before invoking AI, sufficient engineering context shall be assembled.

Context may include:

- Requirements
- Architecture
- ADRs
- Engineering Standards
- Existing Code
- Documentation
- Knowledge Assets
- Previous Decisions

Incomplete context increases engineering risk.

---

# Prompt Engineering

Prompts should contain:

- Objective
- Context
- Constraints
- Standards
- Expected Output
- Success Criteria

Prompt templates should be standardized wherever possible.

---

# AI Validation

Every AI-generated artifact shall be validated.

Validation includes:

- Functional correctness
- Standards compliance
- Architecture alignment
- Security review
- Documentation quality
- Traceability verification

AI output shall never bypass validation.

---

# AI Quality Gates

AI-generated work shall pass the same quality gates as human-generated work.

Quality gates include:

- Engineering Review
- Architecture Review
- Security Review
- Testing
- Documentation Review
- Traceability Verification

There are no reduced quality standards for AI-generated work.

---

# AI Traceability

Every AI contribution shall record:

- Model used
- Prompt version
- Context references
- Output version
- Human reviewer
- Approval status
- Timestamp

Engineering history shall remain auditable.

---

# AI Knowledge Capture

Useful AI outputs shall become organizational knowledge.

Examples:

- Reusable prompts
- Engineering patterns
- Coding templates
- ADR drafts
- Design patterns
- Automation scripts
- Lessons learned

Knowledge capture prevents repeated engineering effort.

---

# AI Governance

AI usage shall comply with:

- Engineering Standards
- Security Standards
- Privacy Requirements
- Compliance Requirements
- Platform Governance
- Ethical AI Principles

Governance applies equally to internal and external AI providers.

---

# AI Limitations

AI shall not be treated as an authoritative engineering source.

Human review is mandatory for:

- Architecture
- Security
- Production changes
- Compliance decisions
- Business-critical logic
- Safety-critical systems

Engineering responsibility always remains with humans.

---

# FORGE Responsibilities

FORGE orchestrates AI-assisted engineering by:

- Selecting workflows
- Providing engineering context
- Invoking AI models
- Managing prompt libraries
- Recording traceability
- Enforcing quality gates
- Capturing engineering knowledge
- Updating registries
- Generating engineering metrics

FORGE acts as the Engineering Orchestrator rather than the engineering decision-maker.

# Summary

The AI-Assisted Workflow establishes a disciplined framework for integrating Artificial Intelligence into engineering activities across AEVON.

By defining clear responsibilities, governance, validation processes, and collaboration models, this workflow enables AI to accelerate engineering while preserving quality, traceability, accountability, and long-term maintainability.

It positions AEVON as an AI-native engineering platform where Human Engineers, AI Engineers, and FORGE work together to deliver reliable, scalable, and future-ready engineering outcomes.
