# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Platform Evolution Standard for the AEVON Platform.

Platform Evolution defines the governance, architecture, engineering practices, and strategic processes required to continuously modernize, expand, optimize, and transform enterprise software, AI systems, cloud infrastructure, and digital capabilities while preserving operational stability and business continuity.

The objective is to ensure that the AEVON Platform remains technologically current, scalable, secure, and aligned with long-term enterprise objectives.

---

This standard applies to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Software Platforms
- AI Models and Agents
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Data Platforms
- APIs
- Integration Services
- Security Architecture
- DevOps Toolchains
- Enterprise Operations

# Vision

To establish a continuously evolving enterprise platform capable of adapting to changing technologies, business requirements, regulatory expectations, and emerging innovation opportunities without compromising operational excellence.

# Platform Evolution Principles

Platform evolution shall be:

- Strategic
- Incremental
- Evidence-Based
- Secure
- Automated
- Business-Aligned
