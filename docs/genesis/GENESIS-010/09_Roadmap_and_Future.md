# Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Technology Roadmap and Future Architecture Standard for the AEVON Platform.

The roadmap defines the strategic direction for platform growth, enterprise capability expansion, AI evolution, technology modernization, research initiatives, and architectural transformation over multiple planning horizons.

Its purpose is to ensure that every technology investment supports long-term business objectives while maintaining architectural integrity, operational excellence, and sustainable innovation.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Software Platforms
- AI Systems and Agents
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Data Platforms
- Security Architecture
- DevOps Ecosystem
- Digital Business Services
- Research & Development
- Technology Portfolio Management

---

# Vision

To establish AEVON as a continuously evolving, AI-first, cloud-native enterprise platform capable of adapting to future business, technology, and regulatory changes through disciplined strategic planning and architectural governance.

---

# Strategic Principles

Future planning shall be:

- Business-Driven
- AI-First
- Cloud-Native
- Modular
- Secure-by-Design
- Data-Centric
- Sustainable
- Innovation-Oriented

Every roadmap initiative shall deliver measurable business and technical value.

# Objectives

The roadmap shall:

- Define long-term technology direction.
- Prioritize strategic initiatives.
- Guide enterprise investments.
- Accelerate AI adoption.
- Improve architectural maturity.
- Support business scalability.
- Reduce future technical debt.
- Enable continuous transformation.

# Planning Horizons

Technology planning shall consider:

| Horizon | Timeframe | Focus |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Horizon 1 | 0–12 Months | Stabilization and Optimization |
| Horizon 2 | 1–3 Years | Expansion and Modernization |
| Horizon 3 | 3–5 Years | Transformation and Innovation |
| Horizon 4 | Beyond 5 Years | Emerging Technologies and Future Vision |

Planning horizons shall be reviewed annually.
