# AEVON

> **Engineering Intelligence Platform**

---

# Vision

Aevon is a long-term Engineering Intelligence Platform designed to help humans engineer, automate, understand, document, verify, and continuously improve software systems.

Its ultimate vision is to become a governed, self-evolving engineering platform capable of proposing improvements to itself while remaining under permanent human approval.

# Engineering Principles

Aevon follows these core principles:

- Architecture before implementation.
- Documentation before coding.
- Specifications before development.
- Contract-first engineering.
- Human-governed evolution.
- Knowledge preservation.
- Provider independence.
- Long-term maintainability.
